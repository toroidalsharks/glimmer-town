# History

Glimmer Town grew one version at a time as a single HTML file, each version patched on top of the last. In v26 the file was split into `src/` so it can be edited like a normal project. The split itself lost nothing: before any v26 fix went in, building `src/` gave back the v25 file byte for byte.

The files in `src/js` are roughly in the order things were added.

| Version | What came in | Where it lives now |

## Nightly changes

- 2026-09-26: Put the project folders back after a phone upload flattened them. At Milo's request, Mili now wears a wispy, medium-length layered pixie cut (a new "Pixie" style anyone can pick), a one-time fix restores it on existing saves, and random morning haircuts no longer touch real people (Mili, Red, Tim, invited residents). Two new smoke checks cover both.
- 2026-09-26: Styles, faces and uniforms. Every resident has a personality (energy, warmth, edge, tidiness, love of cute things) and a clothing style that grows out of it: casual, preppy, sporty, streetwear, emo, goth, alt, jirai kei, gyaru, lolita, cottagecore, Y2K, classic, mom or dad. Faces match (sleepy, sharp, sparkly or dot eyes, lashes, eyeliner, under-eye blush, freckles, cat mouths, resting frowns). Everyone changes into a uniform for their job. Residents comment on each other's outfits, bond over shared styles, and sometimes start dressing like their best friend; the AI minds now know each resident's vibe and what everyone is wearing. Real people start in a plain style and only change when Milo picks one.
- 2026-09-26: Murders now happen about once a real year instead of once a game year (every couple of hours); the Court tab has a picker for it. A grieving detective hands the case to an acting detective. Misleading clues get a follow-up that crosses them out, every clue is labeled (evidence, witness, rumor, ruled out), suspects show how much evidence points at them, and a Detective's briefing scene walks through the case (it also plays by itself halfway through serious cases). Text threads have an exit button at the top and bottom. Auto-update now checks the published page itself before reloading, so an out-of-step version.json can't cause reload loops.
- 2026-09-26: Sentencing. After a guilty verdict the Creator picks a sentence that fits the crime (community service in an orange vest, a public apology, a warning, the cell, probation with an ankle monitor, anger management, stay-away orders, losing a job for fraud; for murder a real year with monthly parole hearings, life, or exile). Parole hearings play as a court scene with the victim's loved one. Wrongly convicted people are paid back. AI-written trial and questioning lines can no longer state facts: they only carry feelings and arguments, and a filter drops any line that mentions times, places, colors, objects or people outside the case.
- 2026-09-26: Look studio. Every resident card has a Change look button near the top (the old style picker was a collapsed row halfway down the card, and it vanished when the cute look was off). It has four tabs: Style, Clothes, Hair and Face. There are 12 new styles (comfy, business, dark academia, coquette, boho, fairy kei, kidcore, neko with cat ears and a tail, e-girl/e-boy, grunge, witchy, techwear), for 27 in all, and every piece can be changed on its own: layers (flannel, puffer, tech jacket, corset, cape, overalls and the old ones), bottoms (cargo, ripped jeans, plaid skirt, tutu), tights, shoes, platforms, hats and hair pieces (beanie, witch hat, cat ears, flower crown, ribbon, star clips, headphones), glasses or sunglasses, neckwear, bags, bangles, a tail, leg warmers and shirt prints. Faces gained eye shapes (starry, cat, droopy, wide, tiny), eye colors, fang and tongue-out mouths, grin and small mouths, brow and blush controls, lipstick, stickers, a bandage, a beauty mark and piercings, plus skin tone and ears. Face changes stay put across style changes, and kids only get kid styles and no makeup. A one-time upgrade moved a few residents into the new styles, and now and then a resident swaps a piece of their outfit on their own. Six new smoke checks.
- 2026-09-26: The workshop's color picker kept jumping back to mint, because the panel redraws itself every few seconds and the dropdown forgot what you chose. It's now a row of color dots that remembers your pick, and every "Make anything" card shows that color. One new smoke check.
- 2026-09-26: Custody and smoother menus. Anyone charged with a crime now waits for trial in the cell under Glimmer Hall instead of walking around town, and a trial that got lost because the box reloaded mid-queue is held again the next morning (or at boot). A verdict whose sentence never got picked uses the default sentence. Mail: going back from a letter returns you to the same spot in the list, open letters have newer/older arrows, swiping, arrow keys and a "Next unread" button. Letters come at most two a night, small ones no more than one per resident every few days and never the same kind twice in a row, each kind has several wordings, and most letters end with a P.S. from the resident's own day (their job, a friend, their outfit, a question for the Creator). The AI letter writer sees their recent days and their last letters so it doesn't repeat them. Scenes no longer close the panel while you're dressing someone or reading mail (the panel counts as busy for 4 minutes in those, 90 seconds otherwise, up from 25), and a panel a scene did close comes back afterwards. The tab row is one scrolling line instead of four wrapped rows, and a ⤢ button makes the panel taller. Five new smoke checks.
